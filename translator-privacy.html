Translator - Python Optic — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Translator - Python Optic ("the app") is published by Python Optic Inc. The app does not collect, store, or sell your personal data. It has no user accounts, no advertising, and no analytics.

Live translation

Live translation is provided by third-party AI services (Google Gemini and OpenAI). To use it, you enter your own API key for the service in the app's Setup screen. Your key is stored only in your device's Keychain and is never sent to us.

When live translation is on, your speech audio is sent directly from your device to the provider whose key you entered, so it can be translated. That processing is covered by that provider's privacy policy (Google or OpenAI). We never receive your audio, your translations, or your keys.

On-device translation

The app's on-device mode performs speech recognition and translation on your device. In this mode no audio or text leaves your device.

Conversation history

Conversations are stored only on your device so you can review past sessions. You can delete them in the app at any time. We have no access to them.
